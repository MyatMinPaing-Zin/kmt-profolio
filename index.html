Who I Am

I am Min Thu, a director, systems thinker, and market strategist with a career built on solving complex, cross-border challenges at the intersection of government, labour, and technology. My work spans founding enterprise-grade digital systems for Myanmar's labour sector, leading market intelligence at a regional travel agency, and driving operational strategy for Spring Wave Company Limited.

What sets me apart is the rare ability to bridge policy requirements and technical delivery — I have shepherded government-scale projects from concept to nationwide deployment without writing a single line of code myself, instead assembling the right teams, aligning stakeholders, and holding the vision together.

I thrive in ambiguity, work across cultures, and consistently turn data into decisions that matter.
